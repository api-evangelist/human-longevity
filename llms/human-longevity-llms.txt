> Human Longevity offers the most comprehensive executive health assessment in the world, combining full-body MRI, whole genome sequencing, 120+ blood biomarkers, and AI-powered analysis to detect disease before symptoms appear. Founded in 2013 by Dr. J. Craig Venter, the scientist who first sequenced the human genome.
